- `npm run wiki:compliance` now checks wiki history as well as commit shape. It compares each note at the branch's base with HEAD and refuses a changed `created` or `agent`, an edit after the `updated` day that leaves `updated` alone, an `updated` later than the edit, and a decision or session body edit, rename, deletion or metadata change after its `created` day other than supersession or a `related` or `tags` change. A shallow clone or an unreadable earlier note exits 2 instead of passing (S22).
