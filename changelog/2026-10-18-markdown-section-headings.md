- `wiki:lint` counts a required section only when it is a visible, top-level level-2 Markdown heading with exactly the section name. Headings inside HTML comments, fenced or indented code, blockquotes or lists, or at another level, no longer satisfy memory-note, work-note or job-spec sections. `## Fix ##` and a setext `Fix` heading now count. The wiki validators and the skill reference checker share one markdown-it configuration in `scripts/lib/markdown.ts` (S20).
