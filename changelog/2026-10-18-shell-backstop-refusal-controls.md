- The merge and push backstop hook (`scripts/claude/block-pr-merge.ts`) now refuses a merge, approval or push to main in an `if`, `elif`, `while` or `until` condition, inside a `$(...)` or backtick substitution in an unquoted heredoc body, and a push with `--repo` or `--repo=<r>` whose positionals name main; quoted or escaped heredoc data still passes. It finds heredocs in linear time, so an opener without a delimiter no longer makes a long command quadratic, and its timing test compares growth between two input sizes instead of a wall-clock bound (S25, audit A22).
